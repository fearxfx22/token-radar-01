How the value is worked out

Every ranked protocol is scored by the same function, from the snapshot’s own figures only: TVL, age, category. No protocol name is special-cased. The implied value is an expected value: the value if a token launched, times the odds that one ever launches.

implied = TVL × fitted multiple(TVL) × P(launch)

The diagram below walks through the top protocol, so you can check every step against the snapshot.

  • fitted multiple(TVL): exp(−1.30 − 0.48 × (ln TVL − 17.30)). A ridge regression of ln(market cap / TVL) on ln TVL, fitted on the 128 live protocols with a positive market cap. The market pays less per TVL dollar for the biggest protocols, and the fit says so with a slope, not a guess: at the median TVL ($32.6M) the multiple is 0.27; a protocol ten times the median TVL gets 0.09; one ten times smaller gets 0.82.
  • P(launch): the forward odds that a protocol which has run this long with no token ever launches one, from peer history inside the snapshot. Among comparable protocols, what fraction already has a token, by age band? The cross-sectional fraction rises with age (0.33 under a year, 0.45 one to three years, 0.79 over three years), so an old protocol that still has no token is less likely to ever launch, not more. The odds are (eventual share − share launched by this age) ÷ (1 − share launched by this age), floored at 0.25 and capped at 0.90. A category with fewer than eight protocols borrows the market-wide rates.

Validation: does the fitted model beat the old one out of sample?

Repeated 5-fold cross-validation, twenty repeats (100 folds), on the 128 labeled live protocols: fit on the training folds, predict the held-out fold. The fitted line cuts the error on ln(market cap / TVL) from 2.35 (radar-2’s category medians) to 2.09, an 11% cut. Ranked by implied value against realized market cap, the fitted multiple alone scores Spearman 0.43 against radar-2’s 0.40. Walk-forward by listing date (train on protocols listed before the cutoff, test on those listed after) favors the full radar-3 model with odds on all four cutoffs: 0.41 / 0.37 / 0.30 / 0.08 against radar-2’s 0.36 / 0.33 / 0.27 / −0.09.

The honest limit: the launch odds cannot be validated out of sample on this snapshot, because no launch dates are recorded, so there is no held-out launch to predict. The random cross-validation above applies the odds to protocols that already launched, where the odds should be 1, so it penalizes them by construction (Spearman 0.39 with odds, 0.43 without). The odds are a peer-history estimate, kept because the value is an expected value and because how long a protocol has run with no token is a figure the value must follow from — not a validated prediction.

What changed from radar-2, and why: the per-category medians are gone (most categories had fewer than five live protocols with a market cap, so most rows were priced on the overall median anyway, and the medians that did exist were noisy). The hand-set growth, breadth and maturity multipliers are gone (they added no held-out rank accuracy: radar-2 scored Spearman 0.401 with them and 0.408 without). Growth in particular is unstable across time — its fitted slope flips sign between walk-forward trains — so the 7-day change is shown on the board as a fact and no longer priced. Age now enters through the launch odds, in the direction the data supports.

What is ranked, and what is not

  • Ranked (206): all 40 watchlist protocols, plus every pre-token protocol below the $50M floor that passes the board’s exclusion rules: no CEX or Canonical Bridge category (an exchange and an arm of a chain, not launches of their own); no versioned name; no whole word wrapped, staked or staking; no exchange-prefixed name. Bridges, liquid-staking, liquid-restaking and restaked-BTC protocols are ranked: they are standalone launches of their own.
  • Not ranked: protocols that already trade (shown in Live tokens at their market caps), wrapped/staked products of tokens that already trade, and the 20 above-floor protocols the watchlist leaves out (shown in Set aside).
  • The honest caveat: “records no token” is what the snapshot says, not a promise that no token exists. An implied value is this radar’s claim about what a launch could be worth, priced on the live market, but a claim all the same. Farm plans assume no token is confirmed.

Farm plans

Every ranked protocol carries an eight-step plan, derived from its own record (category, first chain, TVL, age), never from a hand-written list of names. Capital is tiered by TVL ($50 / $100 / $150 / $250); gas is priced per chain (Ethereum ~$8, Solana ~$0.05, Binance ~$0.10, other chains ~$0.50); the hold is 60 / 45 / 30 days by age. Each step states its action, its cost and its time.
