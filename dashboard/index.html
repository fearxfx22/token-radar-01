How the value is worked out

Every ranked protocol is scored by the same function, from the snapshot’s own figures only: TVL, seven-day growth, age, chains, category. No protocol name is special-cased.

implied = TVL × multiple(category) × growth × breadth × maturity

The diagram below walks through the top protocol, so you can check every step against the snapshot.

  • multiple(category): the live market’s price per TVL dollar in that sector: the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the live protocols in the category that have a market cap. A category with fewer than five live comparables uses the overall live median, 0.1689.
  • growth: 1 + (clamped 7-day change ÷ 100) × 0.5. The change is clamped to −60%…+120% so one wild week (the snapshot’s largest is +4,448%) cannot dominate the board. No 7-day figure means 0%.
  • breadth: 1 + 0.02 per chain beyond the first, capped at 1.10. A protocol live on more chains has more distribution surface for a token.
  • maturity: 0.6 + min(age, 1,095 days) ÷ 1,095 × 0.4. Older with no token means a longer record of value accruing without a launch; it saturates at three years. Age unknown means 365 days.

What is ranked, and what is not

  • Ranked (178): all 40 watchlist protocols, plus every pre-token protocol below the $50M floor that passes the watchlist’s own exclusion rules: no CEX, Bridge, Canonical Bridge, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking or Restaked BTC category; no versioned name; no whole word wrapped, staked or staking; no exchange-prefixed name.
  • Not ranked: protocols that already trade (shown in Live tokens at their market caps), wrapped/staked/bridge products of tokens that already trade, and the 20 above-floor protocols the watchlist leaves out (shown in Set aside).
  • The honest caveat: “records no token” is what the snapshot says, not a promise that no token exists. An implied value is this radar’s claim about what a launch could be worth, priced on the live market, but a claim all the same. Farm plans assume no token is confirmed.

Farm plans

Every ranked protocol carries an eight-step plan, derived from its own record (category, first chain, TVL, age), never from a hand-written list of names. Capital is tiered by TVL ($50 / $100 / $150 / $250); gas is priced per chain (Ethereum ~$8, Solana ~$0.05, Binance ~$0.10, other chains ~$0.50); the hold is 60 / 45 / 30 days by age. Each step states its action, its cost and its time.
